  • Lift is the with-skill score minus the without-skill score. Each score is the mean of its trials, and n is the number of trials per arm (with/without).
  • Evidence: the {{pct .Confidence}} CI is the range the true lift most likely lies in. A lift counts only when the whole range is above zero (or, for a drop, below zero); a range that includes zero is within noise.
  • Helps at lift {{signed .Helps}} or more with the range above zero. Hurts at {{signed .Hurts}} or less with the range below zero. Otherwise Neutral, or Obsolete when the without-skill score is already {{num .Obsolete}} or more.
  • Stable needs lift {{signed .Promote}} on every required model, the range above zero, and no regression. Stable is removed at {{signed .Demote}} or less (range below zero) on any required model. A run without trial scores (an older runner) uses the lift bands alone.
  • Tiers: T1 static, security and quality checks, T2 deduplication, T3 live trials with and without the skill. T1 and T2 run first; if either fails, T3 is skipped and no trials are spent. Markers: filled circle passed, square failed, triangle incomplete (a check could not produce trustworthy evidence), hollow circle skipped.
  • Rejected: Tier 1 or Tier 2 failed on a required model. The version never gets beta or stable, and loses stable if it had it.
  • Publication is SkillEvaluator's assessment across all three tiers: passed, failed or incomplete.
  • Regressed: the with-skill score fell more than {{num .Regression}} below the stable version on a required model, and the {{pct .Confidence}} CI of the difference is entirely below zero. The version keeps beta and does not replace stable.
  • Inconclusive: it fell by more than {{num .Regression}}, but the range of the difference includes zero, so the drop may be noise. The version is not promoted, stable stays, and it keeps beta.
