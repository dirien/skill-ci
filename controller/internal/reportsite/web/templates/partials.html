  • Lift is the with-skill score minus the without-skill score.
  • Helps at lift {{signed .Helps}} or more. Hurts at {{signed .Hurts}} or less. Otherwise Neutral, or Obsolete when the without-skill score is already {{num .Obsolete}} or more.
  • Stable needs lift {{signed .Promote}} on every required model and no regression. Stable is removed at {{signed .Demote}} or less on any required model.
  • Tiers: T1 static, security and quality checks, T2 deduplication, T3 live trials with and without the skill. T1 and T2 run first; if either fails, T3 is skipped and no trials are spent. Markers: filled circle passed, square failed, triangle incomplete (a check could not produce trustworthy evidence), hollow circle skipped.
  • Rejected: Tier 1 or Tier 2 failed on a required model. The version never gets beta or stable, and loses stable if it had it.
  • Publication is SkillEvaluator's assessment across all three tiers: passed, failed or incomplete.
  • Regressed: the with-skill score fell more than {{num .Regression}} below the stable version on a required model. The version keeps beta and does not replace stable.
